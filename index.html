About the Course

Understanding health through evidence and data

Epidemiology and Medical Statistics are essential areas of health science that help us understand disease patterns, identify risk factors, evaluate health interventions, and make evidence-based decisions.


This website provides a central place for our class to access learning materials, explore important concepts, review statistical methods, and keep up with course announcements.
